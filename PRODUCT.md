# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (+ a little vanilla JS). No build step. Chosen by the user over Astro and React/Vite.

## Users

- People living a hard moment (mental distress, addiction, isolation, illness, loss, fragility) and their families, mostly from Castiglione delle Stiviere and the Alto Mantovano, who look for someone who "has been there". Often arrive from a phone, often in the evening, often hesitant.
- People who have already been through it and want to put their lived experience at the service of others (future peer supporters / volunteers).
- Local services, schools, institutions and companies looking for a partner on peer support projects.

## Product Purpose

Website of **Lived Experiences**, an association (associazione di promozione sociale, newly constituted) based on peer support: people with lived experience support people who are going through something similar. The site must make the association understandable, make it easy and safe to ask for help, and turn sympathisers into members, volunteers and donors. Reference model for structure: associazionelibra.com (association site with Noi / Servizi / Progetti / Sostienici / Contatti, membership, volunteering, 5x1000, newsletter).

## Positioning

Help that comes from people who have walked the same road: not professionals talking from above, but "esperti per esperienza" walking alongside.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Real data available: only the name "Lived Experiences" and the registered address above.
- **Placeholders (to be replaced by the user):** phone, email, social links, fiscal code for 5x1000, IBAN for donations, membership fee amounts, office hours, people/team, project names. They are marked in the code with `data-placeholder` / `TODO` comments and listed in README.
- Forms (contact, membership, newsletter) have no backend yet: front-end only, ready to be wired to a service (Formspree, Netlify Forms, etc.).
- Not an emergency service: the site must always point to 112 / emergency numbers for crises.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Lived Experiences" (repo: livedexperience). No logo yet.
- Voice: Italian, warm, direct, peer-to-peer ("tu"), never clinical, never pietistic.

## Evidence on Hand

- No testimonials, numbers, partners or press exist yet. None may be invented as real: any example story must be labelled as illustrative.
- Photos: stock imagery (Unsplash), credited in README.

## Product Principles

1. Asking for help must be possible in one tap from anywhere on the site.
2. Speak as peers, not as an institution.
3. Never fake proof: the association is new, honesty is its credibility.
4. Mobile first: most visitors arrive from a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Readable type for tired eyes, high contrast, reduced-motion respected, keyboard navigation.
